Routing rules

Rules in the router that change where a person ends up.

* “Check my eligibility” picks a destination

Not signed in
#/login
No profile yet
#/details
Has a loan
#/home
Otherwise
#/result

The face check uses the same rule for its next step.

A refresh signs you out

Reloading any signed-in screen logs the session out and opens #/login. Only welcome, terms, privacy, kyc-return and pay-return keep the session on reload.

Sign-in guard

14 screens need a session. Without one, or after any 401 from the API, the app goes to #/login.

Search engines see one page

Everything after # is ignored by crawlers, and the site has no robots.txt or sitemap.xml. Terms and Privacy would need real paths such as /terms to be indexed.
